# Oak Lawn Volleyball

A web app for the Oak Lawn Volleyball community to manage open gym signups, view player lists, read the rules, and connect with other players for league play.

---

## What's on the Site

**Home**

A quick landing page with the next upcoming open gym and a shortcut to sign up.

**Rules**

Read the open gym rules before your first session. There are two tabs: General Rules (sign-up and fee, check-in, team formation, game format, 2-and-off, sportsmanship, safety) and Intermediate Open Gyms (upper B level and above, filtered sign-ups, player caps by court count, King of the Court and ladder formats).

**Schedule**

Browse upcoming open gym sessions with the date, time (Central), location, court details, cost, and a live spot count. Click the spot count to jump to that session's player list.

- Sessions with "Intermediate" in the name are for B Level and above. Everyone can see them, but you need a B Level or higher skill set on your profile to sign up.
- If a session is full, join the waitlist and you'll be moved up automatically when a spot opens.
- Once a session's end time passes, it disappears from the schedule.

**League Hub**

A bulletin board for players and teams to connect. Post that you're looking for a league team, a team looking for players, or that you need a league sub, or post something else. Choose a league level (Any, A League, B League, C/Recreational), filter posts by type, and open any post to read and write replies.

**Player List**

See who's signed up for each open gym (including guests and the waitlist). Click any player's name to view their profile.

**Member Profiles**

Create an account (email or Google) and set up your profile: first and last name, phone number (required to sign up for events, only visible to the admin), skill level (A, BB, B, C, or Recreational), bio, and avatar. Choose a mascot icon or upload your own photo (JPG, PNG, GIF, or WebP, up to 2 MB).

**Friends & Messages**

Send friend requests from a player's profile, accept or decline requests, and send private messages to your friends to coordinate for league play or open gym. Unread messages and requests show up as a badge in the nav. You can also message the organizer directly without adding them as a friend.

## How to Sign Up for an Event

1. Create an account or log in, and make sure your profile has a phone number
2. Go to the Schedule and find an open gym
3. Optionally bring a guest (you reserve and pay for their spot) or team up with friends who are signing up on their own, so you can be put on the same team
4. Choose how you'll pay: Pay Now (Venmo or Zelle) or Pay at the Door (cash, Venmo, or Zelle)
5. Certify the age requirement and agree to the Terms & Liability Waiver
6. Confirm your spot

> If the session is full, you'll join the waitlist and be automatically promoted when a spot opens. If you bring guests and there isn't room for everyone, extra guests are waitlisted until spots open.

Use **Manage Group** on the Schedule to remove individual guests without cancelling your own spot.

## Admin Tools

Organizers get an Admin panel to run the gym:

- **Events:** add, edit, end, reopen, and delete open gym sessions (name, date, start/end time, location, spots, cost, courts, court info)
- **Player List:** add players manually (existing members or walk-ins), remove players and guests, edit names, and track who has paid or still owes
- **Squads & Teams:** see tagged friend groups, create manual groups, and shuffle teams while keeping groups together
- **Event Reports:** ending an event saves a report of who paid, who owes, and who didn't show (the 3 most recent are kept)
- **Members:** view and edit member names and skill levels, and see contact info
- **Activity Log:** the most recent 200 sign-ups, cancellations, waitlist moves, and admin actions
- **Settings:** optional A–Z check-in sort for rosters, plus per-event admin notes
